# Evaluation for SF2701: Financial Mathematics, Basic Course (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation: SF2701 Financial Mathematics, Basic Course (7.5 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold: PASS
* **Reasoning:** The course centers on mathematical rigor and theoretical proofs rather than APIs or tools. Core content includes discrete/continuous-time stochastic modeling, the First and Second Fundamental Theorems of Asset Pricing (FTAP), martingale measures, no-arbitrage replication, and the derivation of the Black-Scholes framework. These measure-theoretic and linear-algebraic finance concepts are mathematically dense and non-trivial to self-teach effectively compared to standard software or applied tooling.

#### 2. Transferability: Moderate
* **Reasoning:** The core domain is derivative pricing and quantitative finance. However, the foundational mathematical constructs taught—such as risk-neutral expectation pricing, martingale theory, stochastic differential equations, dynamic programming under uncertainty, and change of measure—transfer directly into broader stochastic control, reinforcement learning (Markov Decision Processes), actuarial risk modeling, and algorithmic trading systems.

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge: High
* **Reasoning:** This is an introductory gateway to quantitative finance. It provides the essential mental map and foundational theory (discrete binomial models, basic continuous limits) required before entering higher-level topics such as advanced continuous-time finance, term-structure interest rate models, stochastic volatility, and rough path theory. It covers less than 80% of what is required for advanced quantitative research, which has high active research depth and a steep skill ceiling.

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job: Moderate
* **Reasoning:** For core Quantitative Analysts (pricing/risk quants) and derivative traders, risk-neutral pricing and the Fundamental Theorems of Asset Pricing are essential theoretical foundations. However, outside of dedicated derivative pricing desks—such as in statistical arbitrage, systematic quant research, data science, or standard software engineering—these specific continuous-time pricing formulas are rarely used day-to-day, serving primarily as background conceptual context.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
SF2701 serves as a high-density, mathematically rigorous entry point that builds the foundational theoretical moat required for quantitative finance and stochastic modeling, though its day-to-day direct utility is focused primarily within derivative pricing and quantitative risk roles.
