# Evaluation for SF2943: Time Series Analysis (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation of SF2943: Time Series Analysis (7.5 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
* **Verdict:** **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course is rooted in advanced mathematical statistics. It focuses heavily on theoretical concepts such as weak/strict stationarity, autocovariance/autocorrelation properties, spectral representations, state-space representations, and analytical parameter estimation (MLE, Yule-Walker equations, Kalman filtering derivations). The 4.5 ECTS written exam tests pen-and-paper proofs and theoretical mechanics rather than library syntax.

---

#### 2. Transferability
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** Core time series principles (autocorrelation, seasonality decomposition, stochastic processes, state-space modeling, forecasting with uncertainty intervals) transfer directly across quantitative finance, econometrics, supply chain forecasting, anomaly detection, IoT sensor monitoring, signal processing, and macro-level telemetry analysis.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge & Domain Depth
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** Classical ARMA/ARIMA and state-space models serve as the foundational baseline before moving into complex non-linear models (GARCH, regime-switching), high-frequency financial modeling, and deep learning architectures (Transformers, State Space Models/Mamba). SF2943 covers foundational linear theory, leaving a vast skill ceiling in modern multi-variate, continuous-time, and deep forecasting methods.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
* **Verdict:** **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** While time series problems are ubiquitous, day-to-day work for generalist software and data engineers often abstracts these mechanics into high-level libraries (e.g., Statsmodels, Prophet, Nixtla) or uses tree-based/deep learning regressors with lag features. Deep theoretical proficiency is primarily a hard requirement in quantitative trading/research, econometrics, and specialized signal/forecasting engineering roles.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
SF2943 provides mathematically rigorous foundations in classical stochastic processes and linear forecasting that are transferable across industries, though daily industry work often relies on automated toolchains unless operating in specialized quantitative research roles.
