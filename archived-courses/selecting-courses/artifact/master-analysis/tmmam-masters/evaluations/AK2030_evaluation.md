# Evaluation for AK2030: Theory and Methodology of Science (4.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation: AK2030 Theory and Methodology of Science (4.5 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
* **Verdict:** **FAIL**
* **Rationale:** The course covers fundamental epistemology, the scientific method, hypothesis testing concepts, falsificationism (Popper, Kuhn), research ethics, and basic statistical reasoning. It contains minimal to no heavy mathematical proofs or pen-and-paper derivations; the conceptual density is qualitative and easily self-taught via textbooks, essays, and standard open courseware.

---

#### 2. Transferability
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** Concepts such as distinguishing correlation from causation, recognizing cognitive and experimenter biases, experimental design, and ethical frameworks apply universally across all engineering disciplines, computer science, data science, academic research, and product validation.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge
* **Verdict:** **Low**
* **Rationale:** The course provides broad introductory coverage of scientific philosophy and ethics rather than building an extensive foundation with an ultra-high skill ceiling in active rapid development. Completing the introductory material covers the vast majority of the philosophy of science necessary for general engineering and applied research contexts.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
* **Verdict:** **Low**
* **Rationale:** The explicit philosophical frameworks (e.g., demarcation problems, paradigm shifts) are rarely actively referenced in daily engineering workflows; they serve primarily as background intuition, research hygiene, and context rather than day-to-day execution tools.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
AK2030 provides highly transferable conceptual context for scientific literacy and research ethics, but its low technical density and qualitative nature make it easily self-taught rather than essential high-friction coursework.
