# Evaluation for DD2421: Machine Learning (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation: DD2421 Machine Learning (KTH, 7.5 ECTS)

---

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
* **Verdict:** **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course emphasizes theoretical and mathematical foundations rather than API usage. It covers formal derivations and pen-and-paper formulations of classical machine learning paradigms (e.g., maximum likelihood, Bayesian inference, Support Vector Machines via Lagrangian duals, kernel methods, Expectation-Maximization, Decision Trees, and basic Neural Networks). The focus is on underlying mathematical rigor rather than framework documentation.

---

#### 2. Transferability
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** The underlying principles—optimization, statistical estimation, supervised/unsupervised objective functions, generalization bounds, and bias-variance tradeoffs—are universal. They transfer across natural language processing, computer vision, quantitative finance, robotics, bioengineering, and general data analytics.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge / Skill Ceiling
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** Classical ML forms the foundational map required to navigate advanced subfields (e.g., deep generative modeling, reinforcement learning, modern LLM architectures). However, 7.5 ECTS covers only core concepts; it represents far less than 80% of what is needed in active research or advanced production ML, leaving a high skill ceiling in a rapidly evolving field.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
* **Verdict:** **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** In industry, practitioners frequently interface with high-level libraries (e.g., PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost) rather than manually deriving dual formulations or custom EM algorithms daily. However, the theoretical intuition provided by the course is necessary for debugging models, selecting appropriate objectives, preventing data leakage/overfitting, and interpreting failure modes.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
DD2421 serves as a high-density, mathematically rigorous entry point that builds the theoretical intuition required to understand modern AI systems, though practical industry deployment will require supplementary tooling and system-level expertise.
