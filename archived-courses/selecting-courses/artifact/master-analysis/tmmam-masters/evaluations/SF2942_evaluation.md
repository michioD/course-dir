# Evaluation for SF2942: Portfolio Theory and Risk Management (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation: SF2942 Portfolio Theory and Risk Management (7.5 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold: **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course relies heavily on rigorous mathematical theory, pen-and-paper proofs, and analytical derivations rather than implementation frameworks or documentation. Topics include convex optimization, quadratic hedging, utility theory, coherent risk measures (e.g., Value-at-Risk, Expected Shortfall), and cash-flow immunization based on advanced probability and optimization theory.

#### 2. Transferability: **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** While the terminology and specific applications are centered on asset allocation and financial derivatives, the underlying mathematical machinery (convex optimization, quadratic programming, utility theory, and quantitative risk modeling under uncertainty) transfers effectively to fields such as energy trading, actuarial science, operations research, and supply chain risk.

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge: **High**
* **Rationale:** It establishes the essential theoretical foundation and mental framework for quantitative finance and risk analysis. However, covering classical mean-variance optimization, cash-flow matching, and basic risk measures represents only a fraction of the domain depth required for advanced quantitative research, where high-dimensional statistics, stochastic calculus, and machine learning methods are necessary.

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job: **High** *(within Target Domain)*
* **Rationale:** For roles in quantitative asset management, risk engineering, pension modeling, and actuarial analysis, the core concepts—such as tracking error minimization, tail-risk metrics, portfolio rebalancing, and duration matching—form the baseline knowledge used daily.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
SF2942 provides a mathematically rigorous, essential foundation in optimization and risk modeling that serves as a non-negotiable entry point for quantitative asset management and actuarial engineering.
