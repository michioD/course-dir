# Evaluation for DD2257: Visualization (7.5 ECTS)

### DD2257: Visualization (7.5 ECTS) Evaluation

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold: **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course centers on scientific visualization rather than standard dashboard/UI tooling. It covers non-trivial mathematical and algorithmic foundations, including scalar/vector field topology, numerical integration for flow field tracing (streamlines/pathlines), 3D volume rendering integrals, spatial interpolation schemes, and transfer functions. These spatial and mathematical algorithms have higher theoretical density than standard API-driven data visualization courses.

---

#### 2. Transferability: **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** The underlying algorithms (spatial data structures, ray marching, interpolation, vector field analysis, multidimensional reduction) transfer directly to adjacent technical domains such as computer graphics, medical imaging, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), computer vision, and simulation software. However, they have limited applicability to general software engineering, web development, or business data analytics.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge: **High**
* **Rationale:** The course establishes core concepts in the scientific visualization pipeline, but the skill ceiling in production/research environments is significantly higher. Advanced practice demands deeper expertise in low-level GPU programming (compute/fragment shaders), high-performance computing (HPC) in-situ processing, and advanced differential geometry/topology that extend far beyond an introductory 7.5 ECTS scope.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job: **Low**
* **Rationale:** Daily industry tasks for the vast majority of software engineers and data scientists involve high-level charting libraries and existing visualization APIs rather than implementing volumetric or topological rendering algorithms from scratch. This knowledge functions primarily as conceptual intuition unless working directly in specialized R&D positions (e.g., medical imaging software, scientific simulation tools, or graphics engine development).

---

### Strategic Takeaway
DD2257 provides rigorous mathematical and algorithmic foundations for spatial and scientific visualization, making it valuable for specialized careers in graphics, simulation, and medical imaging, but largely non-essential for standard software engineering and general data analysis roles.
