# Evaluation for DA2210: Introduction to Philosophy of Science and Research Methodology (6.0 ECTS)

Here's the evaluation of DA2210: Introduction to Philosophy of Science and Research Methodology (6.0 ECTS) according to the given criteria:

**1. "Autodidact" Threshold**
	* PASS: This course has high theoretical density, requiring students to engage with complex concepts in philosophy of science, which can be challenging to self-teach without proper guidance.

**2. Essential Knowledge in Industry**
	* Low: Philosophy of science is not a primary requirement for high-skill ceiling engineering/research roles, as it is more focused on theoretical foundations rather than technical expertise.

**3. Prerequisite Knowledge**
	* High: Understanding the philosophy of science provides foundational material that can help students navigate complex research questions and scientific inquiry in various fields.

**4. Frequently used on the job**
	* Low: While knowledge of philosophy of science can provide valuable context and intuition, it is not typically a core skill used daily by engineers/researchers in real-world scenarios.

**Strategic Takeaway**: DA2210 provides foundational material essential for navigating complex research questions but is unlikely to be directly applied on the job; its value lies more in developing critical thinking skills rather than technical expertise.
