# Evaluation for DD2585: Programmable Society with Blockchains and Smart Contracts (7.5 ECTS)

**Structured Breakdown:**

* **Autodidact Threshold:** FAIL (tool-, API-, or documentation-heavy)
	+ The course focuses on practical applications of blockchains and smart contracts, which can be easily learned through official documents and online tutorials.
* **Essential Knowledge in Industry:** Moderate
	+ While knowledge of blockchains and smart contracts is valuable for engineering/research roles, it's not essential for high-skill ceiling positions (e.g., Compiler/Kernel, Quantitative Research).
* **Prerequisite Knowledge:** Low
	+ The course assumes prior knowledge of programming concepts and data structures; it doesn't provide foundational material.
* **Frequently Used on the Job:** Moderate
	+ Engineers/researchers may use blockchain/smart contract knowledge occasionally, but it's not a core skill used daily.

**Strategic Takeaway:**
While blockchains and smart contracts are increasingly important in industry applications, this course's hands-on approach makes it more suited for learners who already have some foundational programming knowledge.
