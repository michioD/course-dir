# Evaluation for SF2975: Financial Derivatives (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation: SF2975 Financial Derivatives (7.5 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
* **Verdict:** **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course relies heavily on Tomas Björk’s *Arbitrage Theory in Continuous Time*. It is theoretically dense, focusing on continuous-time stochastic calculus, change of measure (Girsanov theorem), martingale representations, Feynman-Kac representations, and PDE approaches to derivative pricing rather than software toolkits or APIs.

---

#### 2. Transferability
* **Verdict:** **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** The underlying mathematical apparatus (stochastic differential equations, Ito's lemma, martingale measures, and PDE connections) is applicable to stochastic control, physics-informed modeling, operations research, and parts of reinforcement learning/diffusion models. However, the specific financial economic principles (arbitrage pricing, risk-neutral measures, market completeness) are domain-specific.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** This course serves as an essential foundational entry point (the "map/moat") for quantitative finance, but it covers classical Black-Scholes and affine/HJM models. The field’s skill ceiling is high; modern industry applications require extensive further depth in rough volatility, numerical PDEs, jump-diffusions, exotic rate modeling, and high-performance calibration methods that extend beyond this initial foundation.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
* **Verdict:** **High** (for Quantitative Researchers/Traders in Derivatives & Structuring) / **Low** (for broader Software Engineering & Statistical Quant roles)
* **Consensus Rating:** **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** For derivative pricing, XVA, volatility modeling, and fixed-income structuring roles, mastery of risk-neutral pricing and continuous-time arbitrage theory is a non-negotiable daily core skill. For statistical arbitrage, systematic equities/crypto alpha generation, and general quantitative software engineering, the theoretical framework is rarely used directly on the job.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
SF2975 establishes an indispensable, high-rigor mathematical foundation in continuous-time arbitrage theory for specialized quant pricing roles, though its daily practical utility is constrained to derivative-focused desks.
