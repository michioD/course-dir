# Evaluation for SA2001: Sustainable Development & Research Methodology (3.0 ECTS)

### Course Evaluation: SA2001 Sustainable Development & Research Methodology (3.0 ECTS)

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
- **Rating:** FAIL
- **Rationale:** The course lacks high mathematical density, pen-and-paper proofs, or deeply abstract theoretical mechanics. The content focuses on high-level sustainability frameworks (e.g., UN SDGs), academic ethics, and basic research methodology guidelines, all of which are readily accessible via introductory texts, guidelines, and online documentation.

---

#### 2. Transferability
- **Rating:** Moderate
- **Rationale:** Conceptual familiarity with research methodology, literature review practices, and broad sustainability frameworks is broadly applicable across academic disciplines and engineering domains. However, because the depth is limited (3.0 ECTS), the material provides general context rather than specialized cross-domain tools or frameworks.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge
- **Rating:** Low
- **Rationale:** The course does not serve as a technical prerequisite or a complex foundational "map" required to unlock advanced mathematical or engineering coursework. It operates as an introductory institutional/broadening requirement rather than a steep technical moat.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
- **Rating:** Low
- **Rationale:** Day-to-day engineering and technical research roles rely heavily on concrete technical competencies rather than the specific 3.0 ECTS theoretical coverage of this course. It offers high-level ethical intuition and general awareness rather than indispensable daily operational skills.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
Treat this course strictly as a lightweight compliance and contextual requirement to satisfy institutional degree criteria, not as a technical foundation for research or industry competence.
