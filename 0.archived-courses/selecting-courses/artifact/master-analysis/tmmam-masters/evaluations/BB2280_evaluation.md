# Evaluation for BB2280: Molecular Modeling (7.5 ECTS)

### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
**Result:** FAIL
- **Rationale:** The course combines conceptual overviews (molecular mechanics, molecular dynamics, DFT, Monte Carlo, QM/MM, docking) with software labs. Rather than rigorous mathematical derivations or pen-and-paper proofs, it primarily focuses on the methodological foundations and running standard computational chemistry/structural biology toolchains. The software workflows, parameter setups, and basic theory are readily accessible via online tutorials, package documentation (e.g., GROMACS, Amber, PyMOL), and standard open textbooks.

---

### 2. Transferability
**Result:** Moderate
- **Rationale:** The specific tooling and domain models (docking, QSAR, force fields) are tightly focused on biochemistry, biophysics, and drug discovery. However, underlying methodologies such as potential energy surface optimization, Monte Carlo sampling, and numerical integration for dynamics transfer across computational physics, materials science, and physical machine learning/AI for science.

---

### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge
**Result:** High
- **Rationale:** The field is in active development (integrating generative AI, equivariant neural networks, and hybrid QM/MM frameworks), and the ceiling is very high. A 7.5 ECTS introductory course covers less than 20% of the advanced statistical mechanics, non-equilibrium dynamics, advanced quantum mechanics, and custom force-field development required for rigorous research or development roles.

---

### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
**Result:** High *(for computational chemists, structural bioinformaticians, and CADD researchers)* / Low *(for general software/biotech engineers)*
- **Overall Rating:** High (Domain-Specific Core)
- **Rationale:** For researchers and computational scientists in drug discovery, enzyme engineering, and molecular design, these modeling principles, simulation workflows, and structural analysis techniques form the baseline daily toolkit.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
BB2280 provides an essential, high-level map of computational chemistry and structural biology that is critical for specialized drug discovery roles, but its application-heavy nature makes it largely self-teachable through standard documentation and tutorials.
