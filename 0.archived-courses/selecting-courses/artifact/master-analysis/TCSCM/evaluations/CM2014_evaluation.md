**Course Evaluation: CM2014 - Simulation Methods in Medical Engineering (7.5 ECTS)**

High theoretical density requires mathematical rigor, complex concepts, and non-trivial understanding that are difficult to learn through online resources alone.

**2. Essential Knowledge in Industry: High**
High-skill ceiling engineering/research roles require expertise in simulation methods for medical applications, making this course valuable for professionals working in quantitative research, bioinformatics, or edge AI with healthcare focus.

**3. Prerequisite Knowledge: Low**
The course does not cover foundational material that is essential before accessing higher-level domain problems. It's a specialized skill set that may be relevant but not necessarily required as a prerequisite for other courses.

**4. Frequently used on the job: Moderate**
Simulation methods are useful in medical engineering, but they might not be used daily by all professionals in the field; it's more about having a solid understanding of simulation principles to inform design and analysis decisions.

**Strategic Takeaway:** This course offers valuable theoretical foundation for high-skill ceiling roles in medical engineering with moderate practical application, making it a good fit for students aiming to specialize in this area.
