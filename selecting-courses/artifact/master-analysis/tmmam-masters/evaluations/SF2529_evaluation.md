# Evaluation for SF2529: Inverse Problems (7.5 ECTS)

### Evaluation of SF2529: Inverse Problems (7.5 ECTS)

---

#### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold
* **Verdict:** **PASS**
* **Rationale:** The course relies heavily on functional analysis, operator theory, Hadamard well-posedness, ill-posed operator equations, and rigorous regularization theory (e.g., Tikhonov regularization, spectral cutoff, Landweber iteration, variational methods). Developing an intuition for infinite-dimensional topologies, compact operators, singular value expansions, and stability/convergence proofs requires significant mathematical maturity that is difficult to self-teach compared to standard numerical recipes.

---

#### 2. Transferability
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** The core mathematical framework of inverse problems—inferring unobservable system parameters from noisy indirect observations via regularization and optimization—is ubiquitous across quantitative fields. Direct applications include medical imaging (CT, MRI, PET), geophysics (seismic inversion), computer vision (deblurring, super-resolution, neural radiance fields), machine learning (implicit priors, diffusion models), and financial econometrics.

---

#### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge / Field Depth
* **Verdict:** **High**
* **Rationale:** The course serves as a gateway to an expansive and deeply technical field. Mastering this 7.5 ECTS material establishes the foundational "map" (regularization, adjoint state methods, ill-posedness), but barely scratches the surface (covering far less than 80%) of modern subfields like statistical/Bayesian inverse problems, learned deep neural network regularizers, PDE-constrained optimization, and active clinical/industrial imaging pipelines.

---

#### 4. Frequently Used on the Job
* **Verdict:** **Moderate**
* **Rationale:** For general software and machine learning engineering roles, day-to-day work uses standardized end-to-end architectures and off-the-shelf optimization tools without requiring explicit formulation of inverse operator problems. However, for specialized research and engineering roles in medical device imaging, radar/remote sensing, geophysical exploration, computational physics, and modern generative imaging research, this mathematical formulation is a critical, actively applied competency.

---

### Strategic Takeaway
**SF2529 is a high-rigor, high-transferability foundational course that equips you with the formal mathematical toolkit necessary to formulate and solve ill-posed reconstruction and parameter-estimation problems across imaging, scientific computing, and advanced machine learning.**
