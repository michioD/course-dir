# Evaluation for DD2448: Foundations of Cryptography (7.5 ECTS)

**Course Breakdown: Foundations of Cryptography (DD2448)**

**1. Autodidact Threshold**

* PASS: High theoretical density, pen-and-paper proofs, complex mathematical rigor, non-trivial concepts hard to self-teach.

**2. Essential Knowledge in Industry**

* Value for high-skill ceiling engineering/research roles: **High**
	+ Relevant to Compiler/Kernel, Quantitative Research, Bioinformatics, Edge AI, and Robotics.
	+ Cryptography is a fundamental aspect of secure coding, data protection, and authentication.

**3. Prerequisite Knowledge**

* High: **Yes**
	+ Foundational material essential for accessing higher-level domain problems in cryptography.
	+ A "map" or "moat" required before diving into more advanced cryptographic techniques.

**4. Frequently used on the job**

* High: **Moderate**
	+ While not used daily, cryptography knowledge is crucial for secure coding and data protection.
	+ Engineers/researchers use this knowledge occasionally to implement security measures and solve problems.

**Strategic Takeaway:** Foundations of Cryptography (DD2448) is a highly theoretical course with high value for industry applications, serving as foundational material that is frequently used on the job in moderate frequencies.
