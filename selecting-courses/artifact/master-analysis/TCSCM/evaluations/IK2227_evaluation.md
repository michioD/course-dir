# Evaluation for IK2227: Network Systems with Edge or Cloud Datacenters (7.5 ECTS)

**Course Evaluation: IK2227 Network Systems with Edge or Cloud Datacenters (7.5 ECTS)**

### Autodidact Threshold
PASS - High theoretical density (complex mathematical rigor, non-trivial concepts hard to self-teach) due to the course's focus on network systems and datacenter architecture.

### Essential Knowledge in Industry
High - Valuable for high-skill ceiling engineering/research roles such as edge AI, robotics, and cloud computing. It requires a deep understanding of complex systems that is crucial for high-end engineering positions.

### Prerequisite Knowledge
Low - The course assumes prior knowledge in networking fundamentals and computer architecture, making it not essential foundational material for beginners in the field. It does not cover 80 percent of what one needs to know about network systems with edge or cloud datacenters.

### Frequently Used on the Job
Moderate - While understanding of network systems with edge or cloud datacenters is important for certain roles, especially those involving edge AI and robotics, it might not be used daily but rather occasionally in tasks related to system design and optimization.

**Strategic Takeaway:**
"Given its high theoretical density and value for high-skill engineering/research roles, IK2227 could be a valuable addition to curricula focusing on advanced networking and cloud computing skills, assuming students have the necessary foundational knowledge."
