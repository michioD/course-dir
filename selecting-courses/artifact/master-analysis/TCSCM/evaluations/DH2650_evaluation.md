# Evaluation for DH2650: Computer Game Design (6.0 ECTS)

**Structured Breakdown**

### 1. "Autodidact" Threshold (Pass/Fail)

- **PASS**: The course involves high theoretical density with a focus on the design principles of computer games, which can be challenging to learn without hands-on experience or direct guidance.

### 2. Essential Knowledge in Industry (High/Moderate/Low)

- **Moderate**: While not as critical for high-skill ceiling engineering/research roles due to its domain-specific nature, knowledge from this course is valuable within the gaming industry and can be applied in game development, design, or related research.

### 3. Prerequisite Knowledge (High/Low)

- **Low**: The subject matter of computer game design is not typically foundational material for broader computing disciplines like engineering/research roles, as it's more specialized and less prerequisite to accessing higher-level domain problems outside the gaming industry.

### 4. Frequently Used on the Job (High/Moderate/Low)

- **Moderate**: Knowledge from this course might be occasionally used in game development pipelines but is not a core skill essential for day-to-day work in general engineering or research roles, unless directly related to game design or development.
